Right Now — No Trade
Price is floating in the middle of the range. Not at the top, not at the bottom. There is nothing to trade off of. You are just watching and waiting for price to reach one of the walls.
What You're Waiting For
Price to travel all the way up to the HIGH line for a sell setup, or all the way back down to the LOW line for a buy setup. Until it reaches one of those two levels — you do nothing. Waiting is a position.
Sell Setup — Trade From the Top
Price travels back up to the HIGH line, touches it, and gets pushed back down. That rejection at the top is your signal. You sell there. Your target is the LOW at the bottom.
Your Stop
10–15 points above the HIGH
If price pushes above that HIGH, your trade idea is wrong. Get out. But give it a little room so a small fake move doesn't knock you out before the real drop starts.
Buy Setup — Trade From the Bottom
Price comes back down to the LOW line where the trendline is also sitting. Two levels meeting at the same spot. Price bounces off both — that is your signal. You buy there. Your target is the HIGH at the top.
Your Stop
10–15 points below the LOW
If price breaks below that LOW, your trade idea is wrong. Get out. But give it a little room so a small dip doesn't take you out before the real bounce starts.

The Rule
Put your stop below the last low
When you are buying, the trade is only valid as long as price stays above that last low. If price breaks below it, the reason you took the trade is gone. That is your exit.
Give It Room
Go 10–15 points below the low
Do not put your stop right on the exact number. Price will sometimes dip just below a low to knock people out, then reverse back up. Give it 10 to 15 points of breathing room on MNQ.
Example
Last low was at20,000
Your stop goes at19,985 – 19,990

The Rule
Put your stop above the last high
When you are selling, the trade is only valid as long as price stays below that last high. If price pushes above it, the reason you took the trade is gone. Get out.
Give It Room
Go 10–15 points above the high
Price will sometimes push just above a high to grab stops before reversing back down. Put your stop 10 to 15 points above the high on MNQ so you don't get knocked out on a fake move.
