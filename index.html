Daily Chart Markup
Every morning. Same sequence. Do not skip steps.
Morning Sequence — In This Order
4H
Start on the 4 Hour
What is the overall trend? Mark the last major high and low. Draw trend lines. This sets your direction for the day.
1H
Move to the 1 Hour
Confirm the same direction. Mark last high and low. Adjust trend lines. If 1H disagrees with 4H — wait. No trade.
15M
Drop to the 15 Minute
This is where your setup lives. Mark last high and low. Find the last candle before the big move in your bias direction — draw a box around it. That is your order block zone.
5M
5 Minute — Entry Timing Only
Do not look for new levels here. This is where you watch for your 3 candle confirmation and pull the trigger.
What to Mark on Every Timeframe
Last High
Most recent swing high before a pullback. Draw a horizontal line.
Last Low
Most recent swing low before a bounce. Draw a horizontal line.
Trend Lines
Connect swing lows for uptrend. Connect swing highs for downtrend.
Order Block Zone
Last candle before the big move. Box it from top to bottom. This is where you wait for price to return.
Which Candle is the Order Block?
For a buy setup — the last pink candle right before price made a big move up. Box it.

For a sell setup — the last green candle right before price made a big drop down. Box it.

When price comes back and taps into that box — watch for your 3 candle entry.
The A++ Setup
One strategy. Repeatable every single day.
Your 5 Step Process
1
Read Higher Timeframes — Set Your Bias
4H → 1H → 15M → 5M every morning. After that — buying or selling today. Pick one. Stay in it all day.
2
Mark Your Order Block Zone on the 15 Minute
Find the last candle before the big move in your bias direction. Box it. This is where price will come back.
3
Wait for the Break of Structure
Price breaks through a key level. Do not enter yet. This just tells you the setup is forming.
4
Wait for Price to Tap Back Into the Zone
After the break, price pulls back and returns to your zone. This feels like it is going against you. It is not. This is the setup.
5
Enter on the 3 Candle Confirmation — on the 5 Minute
Watch the 5 minute. Wait for the 3 candle sequence to confirm direction inside your zone. That is your entry. See the Entry tab for the full visual.
Your Bias System
📈 BULLISH DAY — BUYING ONLY
Higher timeframes show upward structure. Only looking for buy setups. Ignore any urge to sell.
📉 BEARISH DAY — SELLING ONLY
Higher timeframes show downward structure. Only looking for sell setups. Ignore any urge to buy.
Be Mindful Of
→
Entering on the first big move — that is chasing. Wait for the tap back.
→
Flipping your bias mid day because price moved against you temporarily
→
Lunch hour 11am–1pm Eastern tends to be choppy — only trade if the setup is very clean
→
Watching the 1 minute while in a trade — it will make you want to exit every time
→
Trading when price is in the middle of a range — wait for a key level
→

A pink candle pulls price back into the zone. This feels like it is reversing. Do not enter here either. This is the correction. You are still just watching.

Candle 1 — The Break
Price pushes down out of your zone
A strong pink candle breaks below your order block zone. Do not enter here. You are just watching.
Candle 2 — The Tap Back
Price bounces back up into your zone
A green candle pushes price back up into the zone. Looks like it is going up. Do not enter here. This is the correction. Still watching.
Candle 3 — The Confirmation
A pink candle forms and pushes down out of the zone
This is your entry candle. When you can clearly see the body is pink and growing downward — enter while it is forming. Do not wait for it to fully close.
Clearly Going Up
The candle opened and the body is growing upward. Not a small wick — the actual body of the candle is solid green and getting taller. If you covered the right half of that candle and asked which way is it going — the answer is obvious. That is your buy confirmation.
Clearly Going Down
The candle opened and the body is growing downward. Solid pink body getting taller going down. No confusion about which way it is heading. That is your sell confirmation.
Not Your Candle — Wait
If the candle is going up a little then down a little, sideways, or has a tiny body with big wicks — that is indecision. That is not your entry. Wait for the next candle. A real confirmation candle has conviction. You will know it when you see it.
The Risk of Waiting Too Long
Do not wait for candle 3 to fully close
If candle 3 is so strong that you wait for it to close — by the time it closes the next candle is already flying and you missed the entry. Enter while candle 3 is forming when the direction is clear. Not after.
Zone Management
Which zones to keep, which to delete, and how to find them across timeframes
Keep or Delete
Across Timeframes
Delete This Zone
Price fully passed through it and moved on
If price broke through your zone, kept going, and never looked back — that zone is done. Delete it. Keep your chart clean.
Keep This Zone
Price has not come back to test it yet
If price broke away from a zone and has not returned yet — keep it. It is still live. That could be your next entry when price comes back.
Every Morning Ask
Is this zone still close to where price is right now? Yes — keep it. Price is far away and not coming back — delete it. Your chart is your workspace. Keep it clean.
The Concept
You are not looking for the exact same candle shape on the 15 minute as on the 1 hour. You are looking at the same price range on a different timeframe. The zone is defined by the price levels — not the candle.
How to Do It
Copy the price range to the lower timeframe
The 1 hour shows your order block candle went from 20,200 to 20,250. Go to the 15 minute. Draw a box at those exact same prices. Done. The 15 minute will show multiple small candles in that range — the whole area is your zone.
Simple Rule
The zone is a price range not a candle. Copy the top and bottom price of the order block from the higher timeframe. Draw that same box on the lower timeframe.

MNQ Session Guide
ASIA
Asia Session — 6PM to 3AM Eastern
Lower volume. Price tends to drift or make slow moves. Can set up zones for the next day. Trades are valid but moves are less clean. Only take the setup if it is very clear.
LON
London Session — 3AM to 9:30AM Eastern
Volume picks up. Price starts making bigger moves. Good setups can form here. This is where the overnight direction often gets established before New York opens.
NY
New York Open — 9:30AM to 11AM Eastern
Highest volume of the day. Your best trading window. Cleanest moves, strongest trends. This is where the A++ Setup works best. Be ready at the charts before 9:30.
LUNCH
Lunch Hour — 11AM to 1PM Eastern
Avoid trading here. Volume drops off, price chops sideways and fakes out. Even clean setups tend to fail during this window. Step away from the screen.
PM
Afternoon Session — 1PM to 3:30PM Eastern
Second best window of the day. Volume returns. Often a continuation or reversal of the morning trend. Good setups appear here. Stay alert.
CLOSE
Market Close — 3:30PM to 6PM Eastern
End of day profit taking. Moves can be erratic. Be cautious. This is a good time to close positions and review your trades rather than opening new ones.
Ticks & Points
What they mean and what they are worth on MNQ
What is a Tick?
1
A Tick is the Smallest Move Price Can Make
On MNQ price moves in increments of 0.25 points. That smallest increment is called one tick. Price cannot move by 0.1 or 0.15 — it jumps by 0.25 at a time.
2
Each Tick is Worth $0.50
Every time MNQ moves 0.25 points in your direction — you make $0.50 per contract. Every time it moves against you by 0.25 — you lose $0.50 per contract.
3
A Point = 4 Ticks = $2.00
One full point on MNQ is made up of 4 ticks. So 1 point = 4 x $0.50 = $2.00 per contract. When you say price moved 50 points — that means you made or lost $100 per contract.
4
More Contracts = More Per Tick
If you are trading 2 contracts — each tick is worth $1.00, each point is worth $4.00. If you are trading 3 contracts — each tick is $1.50, each point is $6.00.
