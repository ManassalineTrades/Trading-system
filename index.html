The one rule that saves you every time
Wait for the second time around. The first break is the indication — FOMO traders get in, market takes their money, they get wiped. The second push through the level is yours. Never the first. Always the second.
→

Indication
The blueprint. Not your entry. Information only.
What is an indication?
Price breaks a swing high or swing low on the 1H or 4H. It creates a new high or a new low. This is the starting point of a trend. It answers the question: which direction is price headed?
1H — primary
4H — if 1H is choppy
1
You are in consolidation — no swing levels broken
Price is ranging. Highs and lows are equal. You do not trade this. You wait. Watch for a swing to break.
2
Price breaks a swing high or low — indication fires
A new high or new low is created. Do not enter here. Set a price alert at this level. This level is now your entry zone and your future TP origin.
3
The indication gives you two things immediately
Entry zone — when continuation comes, price returns to this level. That is where you enter.

TP level — the level where the indication started is your take profit. You know your whole trade plan before you ever enter.
Do NOT trade the indication
When the indication fires everyone FOMOs in. They buy the top or sell the bottom. The market then grabs all of them. You are not one of those people. Use the indication as information only. Set your alert. Walk away. Come back for the continuation.
→
Swings getting higher? Bullish. Getting lower? Bearish. Not moving either way? Consolidation. No trade.
→
The 1H is your primary. If it is too choppy to see clear swings — move to the 4H.
→
These levels cost money to break. That is why they hold so strong on higher timeframes. Once broken they have momentum.
Correction
Price grabs liquidity. FOMO traders get wiped. You watch on 15M and wait.
Why corrections happen
After the indication everyone FOMOs in. They have positions. They have stop losses. The market sees all of that money stacked up. It goes to grab it. Price pulls back, hits their stops, takes their money. Then the real move starts. That is the correction.
Monitor on 15M
1
Switch to 15M when the correction starts
On the 1H the correction looks like one or two candles. The 15M shows you the structure inside the correction so you actually know when it ends.
2
Track the highs and lows on 15M
Mark them as they form. If the correction is building higher highs and higher lows (on a bearish pullback) — it is still correcting. It is not over. Do not enter.
3
Watch for structure to break on the 15M
When the correction structure breaks — when price stops making those correction highs and lows — that is when you start looking for the continuation. Drop to 5M.
→
The correction does not always go far. Sometimes it is subtle. That does not matter. What matters is whether the structure has broken.
→
The correction is not a reversal. The 1H level has not changed. The trend is still intact.
→
Do not try to trade the correction itself. Watch it. Track it. Wait for it to end.
Continuation
Your entry. The second time around. This is where the money is.
The second time around — every single time
The first break was the indication. FOMO traders got in. They got wiped. Now price comes back to that same level a second time. The weak hands are gone. This is your entry.
1H — your entry zone
5M — entry trigger
1
Price returns to the 1H indication level
The same level that caused the new high or new low. Your entry zone. This level has the strongest momentum because it is what started the move in the first place.
2
Drop to 5M — watch for a lower high to form
Near the 1H level on the 5M you are watching for a lower high to form. This tells you the correction bounce is done. Price is trying to resume the original direction.
3
Price pushes through that lower high — enter
When price breaks through the lower high in the direction of your trend — that is your entry. You do not have to wait for the candle to fully close. When the direction is clear, enter.
SL
Stop loss — behind the 1H level
Above the 1H level on a sell, below it on a buy. Give it room. Do not put it right at the level. If you are wrong, take the loss and move on.
TP
Take profit — where the indication started
The 1H level that created the new high or new low is your TP. That is where buyers or sellers are waiting. If price blows through it — that is a new indication. You can hold longer.
Set it. Do not touch it.
Set your stop and TP before you enter. Then put your hands in your lap. The plan you made with a clear head is smarter than the fear you feel while you are in the trade. Every loss came from interfering with the exit.
