Indication
The blueprint. Not your entry. Information only.
⚠️
THIS IS NOT YOUR ENTRY
When you see the indication fire — do not enter. FOMO traders entering right now are about to get wiped. Set your alert. Walk away. Come back for the continuation.
✓ Real Indication — Trade This Later
Price was consolidating. Swings were equal. Then it broke a swing high with momentum. New high created. This is your blueprint — set alert, wait for correction + continuation.
✕ Fakeout — Do Not Trade
Price broke above the level but came straight back below it. No momentum. No new high held. This is a liquidity grab. If you entered on the break you just got wiped.
What makes it a real indication
Price breaks a swing high or swing low on the 1H or 4H with momentum behind it. It creates a clear new high or new low. Your entry zone and TP are now set. Wait for the correction to finish.
1H — primary
4H — if 1H is choppy
→
Swings getting higher = bullish. Getting lower = bearish. Not moving = consolidation. No trade.
→
Only look back 3–4 days for your swing levels. You do not need years of history.
→
The indication gives you: entry zone (where it broke) and TP (where the break started). Both known before you enter.
Correction
Price grabs liquidity. FOMO traders get wiped. Watch on 15M. Wait.
✓ Real Correction — Wait for It to End
After the indication, price pulls back in an orderly way. On 15M you can see it building structure going up. It is still correcting. Do not enter until this structure breaks.
✕ Fakeout — Looks Like Continuation But Is Not
Price pulls back and looks like it is reversing at the level. But it is still in correction. No lower high confirmed on 5M. Entering here gets you trapped in the middle of the pullback.
Why corrections happen
After the indication everyone FOMOs in. The market sees all that money stacked up. It goes to grab it. Price pulls back, hits their stops, takes their money. Then the real move starts.
Monitor on 15M
1
Switch to 15M when the correction starts
The 15M shows you the structure inside the correction so you know when it actually ends.
2
Track the highs and lows on 15M
Still building higher highs + higher lows (on a bearish pullback)? The correction is not over. Do not enter.
3
When correction structure breaks — drop to 5M
This is when you start watching for the continuation entry. Not before.
Continuation
Your entry. The second time around. This is where the money is.
✓ Real Continuation — This Is Your Entry
Price returns to the 1H level. On 5M a lower high forms near that level. Price then pushes through the lower high in the trend direction. That push through is your entry.
✕ Fakeout — Looks Like Entry But Is Not
Price comes back to the level but no lower high forms on 5M. It just shoots straight through. Or it taps the level and bounces without structure. No lower high = no entry. Wait.
The second time around — every single time
First break = indication. FOMO traders in, get wiped. Second push = continuation. Weak hands gone. This is your entry.
1H — entry zone
5M — entry trigger
1
Price returns to the 1H indication level
Strongest momentum here because this is what started the move.
2
Drop to 5M — lower high forms near the level
This tells you the correction bounce is done. Price is trying to resume.
3
Price pushes through that lower high — enter
No candle close needed. When direction is clear, enter.
